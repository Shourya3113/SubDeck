## 2. Detailed Store Description (Copy & Paste Ready)

**Transform your cluttered YouTube subscription feed into organized, themed category decks.**

Are you subscribed to 50, 100, or 200+ channels and tired of endless scrolling? **SubDeck** brings powerful, native-feeling category folders directly into your YouTube™ left sidebar and subscription feed.

### ⚡ Key Features

* 📁 **Smart Category Folders:** Group channels into custom folders (*Tech & Coding, Gaming, Music, Education, Entertainment, etc.*) right inside YouTube's sidebar.
* ✨ **1-Click AI Auto-Categorization:** Uses on-device AI (Gemini Nano) or smart heuristics to automatically cluster all your subscriptions into curated decks in seconds.
* 🎯 **Custom Feed Filtering:** Click any folder to filter your `/feed/subscriptions` page so you only see videos from those specific channels.
* ♾️ **Infinite Scroll Sync:** Smoothly auto-loads more videos from your chosen category without breaking YouTube's native continuation loader.
* 📺 **Channel Manager:** Search any channel and easily move or assign channels to different folders via the popup dashboard.
* 🚫 **Hide Shorts Shelves:** Keep your subscription feed clean by automatically removing distracting Shorts carousels.
* 🔒 **100% Private & Local:** All your data, categories, and settings are stored locally in your browser. No third-party tracking, no external servers, no ads.
* 📥 **Backup & Restore:** Export and import your category layouts as JSON files to sync across multiple computers.

---

### 🛡️ Privacy & Permissions Transparency

SubDeck adheres to the Chrome Web Store Principle of Least Privilege:
* **Storage (`chrome.storage.local`):** Used exclusively to save your custom categories, folder names, and channel assignments locally on your device.
* **Alarms (`chrome.alarms`):** Used for periodic background checks to keep your folders in sync with new subscriptions.
* **Host Permissions (`*://*.youtube.com/*`):** Needed only to inject the folder sidebar and filter the subscriptions feed when you browse YouTube.
